2. Pursuer default should be 20% faster than prior beginner test: `speedSearch 1.2 -> 1.44`, `speedPursuit 1.9 -> 2.28`. Keep Spark travel at 26 and 45 landing grace ticks. Original `2.6/3.7` remains source-preserved hard baseline.

## Delivery architecture

**Renderer:** The viewport calculates `effectiveCameraZoom = min(userMaxZoom, availableLogicalHeight/(sixRowGaps+cardHeight+padding))`, centers Spark, and clamps follow lag within the safe free margin. The math/physics geometry and Pursuer positions are never zoomed in the authoritative engine. Both Canvas and accessible DOM hit zones use the same projection. The auto-fit zoom may be much smaller than the user-requested maximum on a short phone: priority is guarantee of three row heights each way. Before/after rows that are not generated by the core are ghost scaffolds with NO numbers, no IDs, no correctness, no routes. At the beginning of a run, negative-index below-tower scaffolding makes the approaching red bot legible. View readout shows the effective zoom and count.

**Difficulty:** `intermediateDevLabPreset()` creates source-valid `cc2-dev-lab/v1` settings with `speedSearch:1.44`, `speedPursuit:2.28`, `nearSenseRadius:180`, `clearSightRadius:270`, `trailSenseRadius:90`, `trailLifetimeTicks:360`, `staleContactTicks:18`, `spawnRowsBelow:3`; learner speed 26, Hop14, Return12 and landing grace45. The previous hard `defaultPursuerPreset()` is not changed. Older `cc2-dev-lab/v1` JSON without personality and feedback still imports, using the original relentless+default feedback semantics.

**Personality:** `PursuerPersonalityV1` is optional and independently versioned. The original Pursuer remains default-relentless in direct engine construction; the demo activates curious through the intermediate preset. A dedicated seeded PRNG makes bounded `hesitationChance`, `detourChance`, `noticeTicks`, `frustrationTicks`, cooldown and durations reproducible. All detours are selected from public graph edges at the bot's actual node; no hidden Spark or math access. No zero velocity is passed to the legal chassis; intentional pauses are represented as a controlled no-step bounded by finite tick deadlines. Emotion and counts appear in actual source snapshots, diagnostics and replay. The base finite-sense Brain and BFS frontier are retained as the eventual discovery backbone.

**Audiovisual:** `CUE_CATALOG` maps short original oscillator pitches + colored electrical rings to game core, feature and Pursuer emotion transitions. `CircuitClimbFeedbackAudio` unlocks only upon user interaction; without that, the game remains playable silently. Audio volume, enabled state, VFX enabled/intensity, and personality controls are versioned and import/export. The canvas shows a visible personality face/halo, and per-event rings show source-backed feedback. No effect or sound can grade math, cause capture or move Spark.

## Known boundaries

- A camera showing 3 + 3 rows at only 230–300px viewport height necessarily makes world objects smaller. Review readability/touch size in owner testing; the minimum accessible button sizes remain 52x44 screen pixels. More advanced semantic zoom (enlarging numbers while compressing only vertical row gaps) is a potential later visual-only improvement.
- Showing three rows behind does not guarantee that a Pursuer lagging by more than three rows is always onscreen. A future threat-direction marker, or dynamic fit including bot with text-size limits, can solve this in the next optics gate.
- Pursuer personality probabilistic choices are reproducible and bounded; indefinitely moving Spark cannot be promised eventual capture. Stationary Spark should eventually be captured by the original finite-sense search.
- No external audio asset bank is introduced. Original synth is a first implementation, not the final multi-layer soundtrack.
