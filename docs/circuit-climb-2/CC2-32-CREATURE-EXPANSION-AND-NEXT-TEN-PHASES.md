## Locked product experience
1. At every READY row, three primary platforms retain source-owned correct/incorrect mathematical meaning. The player can ignore an optional creature and continue the ordinary climb.
2. Creature movement is physical, seeded, finite and traceable. No decorative animation may create a gameplay effect or claim a math receipt.
3. Independently solved creature challenges use distinct receipts and cannot edit the primary answer, score twice or create free solved rows.
4. Power results are visually telegraphed and bounded. Harmful effects must have a fair path, readable warning, and a way to avoid or counteract. Never disguise a wrong primary answer as correct.
5. The Pursuer only reacts to sensed, physically legitimate world effects; no omniscient puppet behavior, no downward pathfinding runaway.
6. A creature never changes the Spark movement or difficulty accidentally because it shares graphics/config state with another species.
7. The three-above/three-below camera and accepted Heritage visible platform renderer remain invariant. Both iPhone and tablet layouts must work without script leakage and first-click blackout.
8. Maintain original Speed Grid-derived 112 BPM five-stem reference as the musical foundation. Creature motifs are event-triggered subordinate voices, not replacement tracks; audio settings and accessibility stay functional.
9. Configuration: seeded presets, live source vs visual settings separation, developer-only diagnostics, import/export/reset. Separate special powers to explicit typed capabilities.
10. Preserve Slate / Focus 43 accepted appearance until a later owner-reviewed visual migration. No unrelated Plaza Invaders mechanics, art, or repository files.
